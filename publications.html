Journal Articles & Preprints

Research Papers

01
Probing non-perturbative aspects on particle production in pp collisions with forward-backward correlations using PYTHIA8
R. Agarwala & K. Dey
arXiv:2508.03124 Under Review — Nucl. Phys. A arXiv →

Investigates non-perturbative QCD aspects of particle production in pp collisions using forward-backward multiplicity correlations, employing PYTHIA8 event generator.

02
Evidence of partonic collectivity in ultra-relativistic heavy-ion collisions with NCQ scaling of radial flow
R. Agarwala, D. Basak & K. Dey
arXiv:2512.15026 Under Review — Phys. Rev. C arXiv →

Presents evidence for partonic collectivity in heavy-ion collisions using NCQ (Number of Constituent Quark) scaling of the radial flow observable — probing the quark-level nature of collective flow.

03
Investigating signatures of collectivity in high-multiplicity pp collisions with differential radial flow observable v0(pT) at Run 3 LHC energy
R. Agarwala, D. Basak & K. Dey
Under Review — Phys. Rev. C

Searches for QGP-like collective flow signatures in high-multiplicity proton-proton collisions at the highest available LHC Run 3 energies using differential radial flow observables.

04
Flattenicity dependence of charge and transverse-momentum balance functions in pp collisions at √s = 13.6 TeV with PYTHIA8
S. Dutta Roy, R. Agarwala & K. Dey
arXiv:2610.26058 Preprint arXiv →

Uses flattenicity to separate jet-like from isotropic proton-proton events, and follows how the charge balance function and its transverse-momentum counterpart change between them in PYTHIA 8 simulations at √s = 13.6 TeV.
