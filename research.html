Research

Recreating the Early Universe

Studying the Quark-Gluon Plasma and collective phenomena in ultra-relativistic collisions at RHIC and LHC energies.

Overview

What I Study & Why

Microseconds after the Big Bang, the universe existed as a primordial soup of quarks and gluons — a state of matter called the Quark-Gluon Plasma (QGP). Today, we recreate this state in laboratories like CERN's LHC and Brookhaven's RHIC by smashing heavy nuclei together at nearly the speed of light.

My research sits at the intersection of this extraordinary experimental program and theoretical understanding — using event generators and computational tools to model, predict, and interpret the signatures of QGP formation, collective flow, and non-perturbative QCD dynamics.

Research Areas

Key Topics

⚛️

Quark-Gluon Plasma (QGP)

Searching for unambiguous signatures of QGP formation — particularly in small collision systems like pp, where its existence is debated. Studying thermalization, deconfinement, and the transition from hadronic to partonic degrees of freedom.

🌊

Radial & Anisotropic Flow

Measuring and interpreting collective radial flow using differential observables v0(pT). Applying NCQ (Number of Constituent Quark) scaling to identify partonic collectivity in ultra-relativistic heavy-ion collisions.

📈

Forward-Backward Multiplicity Correlations

Investigating two-particle correlations separated in pseudorapidity to probe the longitudinal structure of particle production and the role of color strings, ropes, MPI, and QCD radiation in pp collisions.

🔥

Collectivity in Small Systems

One of the most exciting open questions in high-energy physics: does QGP form in proton-proton collisions? Probing high-multiplicity pp events for flow-like signatures at Run 3 LHC energies (√s = 13.6 TeV).

💻

Monte Carlo Event Generators

Using PYTHIA8, PYTHIA8-Angantyr, HYDJET++, UrQMD, AMPT, and EPOS4 to simulate high-energy collisions and compare predictions with experimental data from ALICE, ATLAS, CMS, BRAHMS, and STAR.

📊

Fluctuations & Non-Perturbative QCD

Studying event-by-event fluctuations in particle multiplicities, mean pT, and net charges to understand the QCD phase structure and probe the non-perturbative regime of strong interaction physics.
