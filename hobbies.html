Hobbies & Passions

Sport & Inspiration

Cricket — And the Man Behind the #18

🏏 Jersey No. 18 — Forever

Virat Kohli

If Dr. Kalyan Dey shaped my scientific identity and Mr. Manoj Dutta gave me mathematics, then Virat Kohli gave me something equally essential — the will to keep going when things get hard.

Research is not a sprint. It is a grind — long nights of failed simulations, stubborn results that refuse to converge, papers that take months to write. In those moments, I think of Kohli. The man who turned self-doubt into steel. Who came back from fitness issues, form slumps, and captaincy struggles to produce some of the most legendary innings in cricket history. His philosophy is simple and profound:

"Self-belief and hard work will always earn you success."

— Virat Kohli

That philosophy lives in every paper I submit, every conference I attend, and every equation I solve. There is always light at the end of a tunnel — Kohli taught me that. And the jersey number he made iconic? #18. It's in my email address. It's in the way I approach my work: never giving up, always hungry for the next breakthrough.

I've been representing Bodoland University in inter-university cricket tournaments (2022–2024), including the Lt. Nilay Dutta Memorial Inter University Cricket Tournament. The sport keeps me grounded, sharp, and reminded that both cricket and physics demand the same thing: intense focus, patience, and the courage to trust your instincts.

Visual Art

Photography

📸

A physicist studies the universe through equations. A photographer sees the same universe through a lens. For me, photography is an extension of scientific curiosity — noticing what most people walk past, finding the extraordinary in the ordinary, and asking "what if I look at this differently?"

I am always ready with my phone camera. A ray of light streaming through a window, the geometric precision of a building's shadow, the chaos of a busy market frozen in a single frame — I find meaning in all of it. Photography is about capturing moments before they become memories, and I've made it a lifelong practice.

I am also a Google Maps Local Guide — Level 7, with over 2.1 million views on my contributed photos and reviews. That's 2.1 million times someone used a photo I took to help navigate their world. That feels like its own kind of impact.

Laughter & Levity

Stand-up Comedy (Sort Of)

🎤

I have always been the class clown. In lecture halls, in labs, at conferences — there's always a joke forming at the back of my mind, waiting for the right moment to escape. Sometimes it lands beautifully. Sometimes it crashes harder than a Monte Carlo simulation with wrong initial conditions. But does that stop me? Absolutely not.

I believe humor is essential in academia. Physics can be dense, isolating, and relentlessly serious. A good joke can break the tension in a seminar, make a student feel comfortable asking a "stupid" question, or just remind everyone in the room that we are human beings doing science — not machines producing papers.

"Comedy is just physics with a punchline — you set up the initial conditions, let the system evolve, and hope the result surprises everyone."

— Rohit Agarwala (yes, I came up with that)

My friends have a running joke that my physics talks are funnier than my actual jokes. I choose to take that as a compliment.

🌊

"A Researcher Who Goes With The Flow"

In physics, flow describes the collective motion of a system — how particles respond to pressure, gradients, and forces in concert. In life, going with the flow means staying curious, staying adaptable, and trusting that the current will carry you somewhere worth going. That's my mantra — in the lab, on the cricket pitch, behind the camera, and on the makeshift comedy stage.
